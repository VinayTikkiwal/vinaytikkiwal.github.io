Privacy Policy for CBM Calculator

Package: com.funbucket.cbmcalculator Developer: Maritime Global Logistics Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Maritime Global Logistics ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects your information when you use our Android mobile application CBM Calculator & Shipping Freight Estimator.

Zero Cloud Tracking Commitment: CBM Calculator is built with a strict offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not harvest, collect, sell, or rent your personal information, cargo measurements, or freight calculation records to third parties.

2. Information Collection and Storage

A. User-Entered Cargo Data (Stored Strictly On-Device):

  • Data Types: Carton dimensions (Length, Width, Height, Units: cm, inches, m, ft), gross weight, cargo quantities, package names, port codes, container loading presets, and multi-box shipment manifests.
  • Storage Sandbox: All data is stored strictly in your device's private local sandbox storage (SharedPreferences with MODE_PRIVATE and local SQLite Room database).
  • Zero Remote Transmission: Your cargo data never leaves your device and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external server or cloud database.

B. Device Permissions (Least-Privilege Standard):

CBM Calculator requests zero sensitive device permissions:

  • NO access to Precise or Coarse GPS Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION).
  • NO access to Camera or Microphone (CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO).
  • NO access to Contacts or Phone State (READ_CONTACTS, READ_PHONE_STATE).
  • NO access to Broad External Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • Only standard network access permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and com.android.vending.BILLING) are used for optional ad display and Google Play subscription verification.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing 8.0)

The app offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions and upgrades (Quarterly Plan ₹499, Half-Yearly Plan ₹799, and Yearly Plan ₹1,499):

  • Google Play Billing (v8.0.0): All monetary transactions, payments, renewals, and refunds are processed exclusively by Google Play.
  • Zero Financial Data Access: Maritime Global Logistics never receives, sees, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, debit cards, bank details, or UPI credentials.
  • Subscription Management: You can cancel, modify, or pause your subscriptions at any time via the Google Play Store app under Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the application free for all logistics professionals, CBM Calculator integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

Google AdMob may process pseudonymous identifiers and telemetry in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and the Privacy Sandbox:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): A user-resettable unique identifier used for ad frequency capping and fraud detection. You can reset or delete this ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Coarse Location & IP Address: Used to display geographically appropriate ads.
  • Diagnostic & Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and load latency metrics to ensure smooth ad rendering.

For more details, review Google Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information from Apps that Use Its Services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

Because all records remain exclusively on your local device:

  • Individual Deletion: You can delete any saved shipment directly inside the app on the Saved Shipments screen.
  • Complete Purge: You can completely purge all saved data and calculation history at any time by navigating to your Android device Settings > Apps > CBM Calculator > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

CBM Calculator is a professional cargo logistics calculation tool intended for freight forwarders, shipping agents, and business operators aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Calculation & Cargo Loading Disclaimer

Calculations, volumetric weight ratios, and container load counts (20ft, 40ft, 40ft HQ, 45ft HQ, and trucks) provided by the application are mathematical estimates based on standard international ISO specifications. Actual loading capacity may vary due to carton stiffness, palletization, dunnage, packing variations, and carrier highway weight regulations.

8. Security & Code Integrity

We enforce strict security practices including encrypted network communication (blocking cleartext unencrypted HTTP via Android Network Security Configuration) and ProGuard/R8 code optimization and DEX obfuscation to protect application integrity.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updates.
