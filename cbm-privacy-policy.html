Privacy Policy

CBM Calculator & Shipping Freight Estimator
Effective Date: October 27, 2026 | Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy describes how Maritime Global Logistics ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects your information when you use our mobile application CBM Calculator (Package: com.funbucket.cbmcalculator).

We respect your privacy. CBM Calculator is designed with a strict offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or rent your personal data or cargo calculation records.

2. Information Collection and Storage

A. User-Entered Data (Stored Strictly Locally):

  • All carton dimensions (Length, Width, Height, Units), gross weights, cargo quantities, shipment names, and multi-box manifests you input are stored strictly in your device's private, encrypted sandbox storage (SharedPreferences with MODE_PRIVATE).
  • This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any cloud database.

B. Device Permissions:

  • CBM Calculator adheres to the Least-Privilege Principle.
  • We do not request sensitive runtime permissions such as Location (GPS), Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or Broad External Storage.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the application free for all logistics professionals, CBM Calculator integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to serve advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and telemetry to deliver relevant or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance in compliance with Google Play Policies and the Privacy Sandbox:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): A user-resettable unique identifier used for ad frequency capping and fraud detection.
  • Coarse Location / IP Address: Used to display geographically appropriate ads (e.g., regional language or country).
  • Diagnostic & Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and load latency metrics to ensure smooth ad rendering.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Apps that Use Its Services

4. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

Because all your shipment records are stored locally on your device:

  • Individual Deletion: You can delete any saved shipment directly inside the app on the Saved Shipments screen.
  • Complete Purge: You can purge all app data and calculation history at any time by navigating to your Android device Settings > Apps > CBM Calculator > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

CBM Calculator is a professional cargo logistics calculation tool intended for freight forwarders, shipping agents, and business operators aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Calculation & Loading Disclaimer

Calculations, volumetric weight ratios, and container load counts (20ft, 40ft, 40ft HQ, 45ft HQ, and trucks) provided by the application are mathematical estimates based on standard international ISO specifications. Actual loading capacity may vary due to carton stiffness, palletization, dunnage, packing variations, and carrier highway weight regulations.

7. Security

We implement strict security practices including enforced HTTPS via Android's Network Security Configuration (blocking cleartext unencrypted HTTP traffic) and code obfuscation to protect application integrity.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any updates.
