Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Resume Builder: CV Maker PDF mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by the developer team reachable at bucketfun2015@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Your privacy and the security of your professional data are our utmost priority. We operate under a privacy-first model:

  • Resume and Personal Data: All information you input (such as your full name, work history, education, skills, contact info, and profile pictures) is stored locally on your device in a secure database. We do not transmit or store your personal resume data on our servers.
  • AI Resume Generation: If you use optional AI writing features (such as generating summaries or bullet points), prompt data (such as job title or rough notes) is processed via secure Google Gemini API connections solely to generate text suggestions. None of your data is used to train AI models.
  • Imported Documents: If you choose to import a .PDF or .DOCX file, the file is parsed and processed directly on your local device.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To support app maintenance and optional premium features, the App integrates standard, trusted third-party services that may collect device identifiers and usage metrics:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs (GAID), IP address, and interaction data to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Policies.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used to manage VIP and Premium purchases securely. Financial transactions and payment details are processed directly by Google Play and never accessed by our App.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimal permissions required for functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch AI generation requests, manage in-app billing verification, and serve ads.
  • Photo Picker / Storage: Uses the secure, zero-permission Android system Photo Picker when you select a profile picture for your resume.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your resume data is saved on your device's local database, you retain 100% control over your data. You can delete individual resumes or clear the application cache/data at any time from your device Settings to permanently remove all stored information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
