Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026  ·  Package ID: com.funbucket.systematic.daily.planner  ·  Developer: funbucket

Systematic: Daily Planner ("Systematic", "the App", "we", "our") is developed and published by funbucket. We respect your privacy and designed Systematic so your personal daily plans, tasks, routines, and screen-time statistics stay private on your Android device.

Privacy Summary: Systematic does not require account registration and does not operate a personal cloud server for your tasks or app usage history. Your tasks, routines, notes, and screen-time stats remain strictly local on your device.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use Systematic, the following user-created data is stored locally in private app storage on your device:

  • Scheduled timeline tasks, subtasks, recurrence rules, inbox items, and custom routines.
  • Focus session statistics, routine completion streaks, and digital detox check-in logs.
  • App preferences such as selected theme, language, notification reminder settings, and social budget limits.
  • One-time 24-hour rewarded Pro pass eligibility status (stored locally on your device).

When you export a PDF or CSV performance report, the file is generated locally on your device and saved to your device's Downloads folder or shared only with the destination app you explicitly choose.

2. Android Usage Access Permission (Screen Time & Social Media Budget)

Systematic includes an optional Pro feature for Screen Time & Social Media Budget tracking. To enable this feature, you may choose to grant Android's Usage Access permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) in Android System Settings after reviewing our in-app disclosure.

  • What is accessed: Local foreground app usage durations for your phone and selected social/video apps (such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, X, Snapchat, Reddit, Threads, Pinterest, Twitch, LinkedIn, and Netflix).
  • How it is used: Exclusively on-device to calculate your daily phone time, 7-day average, social media budget progress, 80%/100% local limit alerts, and distraction-free focus badges.
  • No transmission or sharing: Your Usage Access data is never uploaded to any server, never sold, and never shared with third parties or advertisers.
  • Control: You may revoke Usage Access at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Special app access > Usage access.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP Consent)

The free tier (and optional one-time 24-hour rewarded Pro pass) is supported by advertisements served by Google AdMob, including Banner, App Open, Post-Routine Interstitial, and opt-in Rewarded ads. Purchasing Systematic Pro removes all ads while your entitlement is active.

Google AdMob and its mediation partners may collect and process limited device and diagnostic information, such as your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, coarse location, app interaction events, and crash/performance metrics, to deliver, cap, and measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • Consent Management (EEA, UK, Switzerland & US States): We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request your consent where required by GDPR and applicable regional privacy laws. You can update or withdraw ad personalization choices anytime inside the App via More > Ad Privacy Settings (where applicable) or in your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from apps using its services at Google Privacy & Terms.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Systematic offers auto-renewing subscriptions (Monthly, 3-Month, 6-Month, Annual) and a one-time Lifetime Unlock through Google Play Billing. All payment transactions, receipts, billing details, and subscription renewals are processed securely by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card, bank, or payment credentials.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

5. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load Google AdMob ads, UMP consent forms, and Google Play Billing product catalogs.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & VIBRATE: Used to deliver your scheduled task reminders, routine alerts, nightly screen-free curfew reminders, and optional social budget alerts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to deliver local reminders reliably at your scheduled times and restore scheduled reminders after a device reboot.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Optional): Used strictly on-device for the optional Screen Time & Social Budget feature as described in Section 2.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your planning data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete individual tasks, inbox items, or routines directly inside the App, or delete all local app data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling Systematic from your Android device. If Android Backup is enabled on your device, you can manage backed-up app data via your Google Account backup settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Systematic: Daily Planner is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the App or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
