### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Habit Hero: RPG Habit Tracker** ("the App"), developed by **funbucket** ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our App.

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles information, what data third-party service providers may process, and your rights regarding your data.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
* **Personal Habits, Tasks, and Routine Data**: All your habits, daily quests, to-do lists, streaks, character level, pets, gold, and game progress are stored **100% locally on your device** using local SQLite database storage. We do not transmit, sync, or store your personal habit records on our private servers.
* **Account Credentials**: You do not need an account or login to use Habit Hero. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or passwords.
* **Location Data**: We do not request, track, or collect your GPS coordinates or precise location.

---

### 3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services
To provide in-app advertising and optional in-app purchases, we integrate trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google:

#### A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:
* Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID).
* IP address and coarse network location (city/country level).
* Ad interaction data (ad views, clicks, and impressions).
* Diagnostic information for fraud prevention and performance analysis.

You can learn more about how Google processes information by visiting [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings (**Settings &rarr; Google &rarr; Ads &rarr; Delete Advertising ID**).

#### B. Google Play In-App Billing
We use Google Play's native Billing service to process optional purchases (e.g., Starter Bundles, Streak Shields, and Remove Ads Lifetime Pass).
* Financial data (credit card numbers, billing addresses) is collected and processed **exclusively by Google LLC**.
* We never see, receive, or store your payment card details.
* The App receives an anonymous purchase token from Google Play to unlock the corresponding in-game perks.

---

### 4. Data Security & Storage
* **Encrypted in Transit**: All network requests initiated by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.
* **Local Database Protection**: Your local database (`habithero.db`) is protected by Android application sandboxing, meaning other apps on your device cannot access your habit data without system root access.
* **Backup Rules**: The App participates in Android Cloud Backup with explicit encryption rules, allowing you to restore your habit progress when switching devices using your personal Google account.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Habit Hero is intended for a general audience aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through third-party ad networks, please contact us at **vinay.tikkiwal1@gmail.com**, and we will promptly investigate and take necessary measures.

---

### 6. Data Deletion Rights
Because your habit and character data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:
* You can delete all your stored data at any time by going to your device's **Settings &rarr; Apps &rarr; Habit Hero &rarr; Storage &rarr; Clear Data**.
* Uninstalling the application will permanently delete all local habit databases and preferences.
