Privacy Policy

App: DeepPause: Screen Time & Detox (com.deeppause.detox)
Developer: Funbucket
Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Funbucket ("we", "our", or "us") built DeepPause: Screen Time & Detox to help you reduce screen time, build healthier digital habits, and stay focused. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses on your device, how it is used, and your choices.

Local-First Privacy Summary: DeepPause does not require an account or login. Your detox sessions, schedules, app limits, blocked websites, streaks, badges, and Swear Jar records are stored 100% locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or sold to third parties.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core features, DeepPause saves your preferences and progress locally in private Android app storage:

  • Active detox timers, completed session history, streaks, and unlocked achievement badges.
  • Recurring detox schedules, Pomodoro timer settings, and Smart Context Break preferences.
  • Your chosen Allowed Apps (whitelist), Per-App Daily Limits, and custom Blocked Website domains.
  • Optional Accountability Swear Jar entries, local leaderboard nickname, and friend invite codes you choose to share.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or messages.

2. AccessibilityService API Disclosure

DeepPause requests Android's AccessibilityService API solely to power its core digital wellbeing and blocking features, and only after you explicitly consent in our in-app disclosure dialog and enable it in Android Settings:

  • App Blocking & Daily Limits: Detects the package name of the currently active foreground app so non-whitelisted apps are blocked during an active detox challenge or paused when a daily app time limit is reached.
  • Website Filtering: Reads the address bar URL of supported web browsers only while a detox session is active to block distracting domains you configured.
  • Anti-Cheat Settings Protection: Optionally prevents opening Android Settings or Package Installer while an active detox challenge is running, if you enable that setting.
  • Smart Break Context Detection: Measures continuous foreground session duration and rapid app switching locally to suggest timely eye and body breaks.

Strict Privacy Guarantee: All AccessibilityService events are processed exclusively on your device in real time. DeepPause never records, logs, transmits, stores remotely, or shares screen content, keystrokes, passwords, personal messages, or browsing history.

3. Other Android Permissions & Why We Use Them

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Reads local app usage totals to display your daily, weekly, and monthly screen-time charts, enforce daily app budgets, and power Smart Context Break reminders.
  • Installed Apps List (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Displays your installed apps inside DeepPause so you can pick Allowed Apps (whitelist), set Per-App Daily Limits, and exclude apps from Smart Break alerts. This list never leaves your device.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Immediately brings back the DeepPause lock screen if a blocked app is opened during an active challenge.
  • Device Administrator (Optional Anti-Uninstall): Prevents uninstalling DeepPause during an active detox session if you choose to enable this protection. You can deactivate it anytime in Android Settings.
  • Do Not Disturb Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Optionally silences distracting notifications automatically while a detox challenge is active.
  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Shows your active detox countdown, triggers scheduled detox routines on time, and delivers local Smart Context Break reminders.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of DeepPause displays ads provided by Google AdMob (including Banner, App Open, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded ads). Google AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, app interaction events, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's policies.

  • Upgrading to a paid DeepPause Pro subscription removes Banner, App Open, and Interstitial ads after any free trial ends.
  • You can reset or limit personalized advertising anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information From Apps.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

DeepPause Pro subscriptions and optional Accountability Swear Jar purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card, bank, or billing address details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

6. Sharing Features

When you tap Share App, share a streak badge image, or share a friend leaderboard code, DeepPause uses Android's standard share sheet so you choose which app or contact receives the content. Shared badge images are temporarily generated in private app cache and never uploaded by us.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your DeepPause data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it at all times. You can permanently delete all local app data at any time by going to Android Settings → Apps → DeepPause → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Children's Privacy

DeepPause is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
