1. Overview

FinMint Calculator ("the App") is developed by funbucket as an ad-supported personal finance and wealth calculator application. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information You Enter in the App (Stored Locally Only)

All calculator inputs (such as loan amounts, SIP investments, interest rates, term insurance details, and tax figures), saved calculation plans, financial goals, and app preferences (language, currency, number format, and theme) are processed and stored strictly locally on your device.

  • We do not require account registration or login.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell, or store your financial calculations, saved plans, or personal financial inputs on any developer server.

3. Permissions Used

FinMint Calculator requests only minimal network permissions:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used solely to load advertisements via Google AdMob and open optional external links (such as rating the App or viewing more apps on Google Play).

The App does not request access to your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Photos/Media, SMS, or Precise Location.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and App Open ads). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information in accordance with Google's privacy policies, including:

  • Device or other IDs (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (inferred from IP address)
  • App interactions, ad impressions/clicks, and diagnostic/crash data

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device via: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Sharing Features

When you tap "Share Plan" or "Share App," the App opens your device's standard Android share sheet with a plain-text summary so you can choose which app or contact to share it with. We do not track or access who you share with.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all saved plans, financial goals, and preferences are stored only on your device:

  • You can delete individual saved calculations or goals inside the App at any time.
  • Clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

FinMint Calculator is a general-audience financial utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can assist.

8. Financial Disclaimer

FinMint Calculator provides mathematical estimates and projections for informational and personal planning purposes only. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Please consult a qualified financial advisor before making financial decisions.
