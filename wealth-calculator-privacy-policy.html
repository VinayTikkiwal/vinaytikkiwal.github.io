1. Information We Collect and Process

A. On-Device Financial Data & Calculations

Information you enter into our calculators (such as SIP amounts, interest rates, loan tenure, income, and financial goals) is processed locally on your device. Your saved calculations and financial goals are stored locally in your device's private database and are never uploaded to our servers.

B. Artificial Intelligence (Gemini AI) Features

Our app offers optional AI-powered features (AI Financial Assistant, Voice Assistant, What-If Scenarios, Document Scanner, and Result Explainer) powered by Google Gemini AI API:

  • Prompts & Queries: Text or voice queries you submit to the AI Assistant are sent to Google's Gemini API strictly to process and generate responses.
  • Document Scanner: Images or text extracted from documents you choose to scan (such as loan statements or financial summaries) are sent to Google Gemini API solely for analyzing values.
  • Note: We do not store or sell your AI conversation history or scanned images on external servers.

C. Device Permissions

Our app requests optional device permissions only when you explicitly trigger specific features:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely for speech-to-text voice input when using the Voice Assistant. Audio is never recorded, stored, or transmitted in the background.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely when you choose to scan financial documents or statements via the AI Document Scanner.

D. Automatically Collected Data & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the application. Google AdMob may collect and process certain data according to their privacy policies:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
